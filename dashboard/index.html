What was actually checked
  • 471 observed within-run attempt pairs, including 152 failure-to-success pairs across 131 distinct runs. No workflow was triggered by this study.
  • Among 87 failed attempts in the fixed 500-attempt audit: 39 test/compile-like, five environment-like, three mixed and 40 unknown.
  • Seventeen static descriptors and six released-evidence history descriptors. B0 already had workflow failure history and run-start clock inputs.
  • Replayed saved models and recomputed AP, Brier and confusion totals on all matched rows.
  • Four original unavailable log archives and one oversized log remain explicit limits. Missing evidence is never turned into a clean label.
Rerun discordance and log signatures are not causal code/non-code labels. There is no consistent per-test history ledger. All nine external projects were already viewed before the extension, so its results are exploratory.
